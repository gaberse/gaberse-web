# Gaberse — Product Blueprint v1

## Product thesis

Gaberse is Gabriela's personal universe.

It is a living index of things she makes, explores, experiences and continues thinking about.

The site should create intrigue first, then understanding, then trust.

It does not need a single explanatory narrative connecting every Item. Authorship is enough.

> Every artifact should reveal a piece of the person behind it.

## Desired first impression

Minimal, editorial, clean, intentional.

Strong typography and whitespace create order.

Motion creates discovery.

Interactive artifacts should feel tactile but subtle.

The site should make people want to continue exploring and make it easy to reach Gabriela.

## Journey

- 5 seconds: see the most important/current Interests.
- 30 seconds: understand what Gabriela is working on now.
- 2 minutes: discover the person behind the work.
- 5 minutes: feel comfortable contacting her if something resonated.

## Homepage

Must:
- Interests
- selected Items
- Archive access
- direct contact

Useful:
- Currently

## Product rule

A new Item should be able to enter the universe without requiring a new content entity type.

Different Items may need custom compositions.

Reuse primitives, not identical page templates.

## Contact
